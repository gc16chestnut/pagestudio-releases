PageStudio Privacy Policy

Applies to the PageStudio desktop program for Windows and the PageStudio Viewer for Android and iPhone. Effective November 2026.

The short version

PageStudio is a desktop publishing program that works on your own computer. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not collect or send your publications or personal information to the developer. Your files stay on your computer or wherever you choose to save them. A few optional features connect to other companies' services, and only when you use them; those are described below.

What stays on your computer

  • Your publications, pictures, recent-file list, settings, saved handwriting, print settings and automatic recovery copies are stored on your computer only.
  • PageStudio keeps small log files about starting up and about errors, to help with troubleshooting. They stay on your computer and are never sent anywhere automatically.
  • If you enter an Anthropic API key for the Claude assistant, it is stored on your computer, encrypted by Windows for your user account. It is only ever sent to Anthropic, with your assistant requests.

When PageStudio connects to the internet

  • Updates. The installed program checks for new versions on GitHub (github.com), where new versions are posted, and downloads them from there. The phone viewer checks GitHub once a day for updates to its pages. These requests share only what any web download shares, such as your computer's internet address, with GitHub. No information about you or your files is included. You can switch off automatic update checks in Options.
  • The Claude assistant (optional). If you use the assistant built into PageStudio, your request goes straight from your computer to Anthropic's service (api.anthropic.com) under your own API key. A request includes what you typed, a description of the publication you have open, and usually a picture of the current page. Anthropic's handling of that information is covered by Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you use the assistant.
  • The Claude desktop app connection (optional). If you connect PageStudio to the Claude desktop app, the Claude app can read and change the publication you have open when you ask it to. The connection runs on your own computer. What the Claude app does with that information is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
  • Canva (optional). The Canva button opens Canva's website (canva.com) in a PageStudio window, where you sign in to your own Canva account. Canva's terms and privacy policy apply to what you do there. Designs you download from that window are brought into your publication on your computer.
  • Pictures from a web address. If you insert a picture from a web address, PageStudio downloads it from that website.
  • Look-up and map links. Commands that look up a word or verse, or make a map QR code, open the site in your web browser (for example a dictionary, Bible Gateway, Bing or Google Maps). That site's own privacy policy applies.

The phone viewers

The Android viewer and the iPhone web viewer open the files you choose and show them on your phone. Your files are not uploaded anywhere. The iPhone web viewer is served from GitHub Pages, and like any website it is requested from GitHub when you first open it or when it updates.

Children

PageStudio is general-purpose software and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If PageStudio adds a feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact
