How it works

  • Print the practice sheets and fill them in with a dark pen. It takes about half an hour.
  • Scan or photograph them. PageStudio reads your letters, letter pairs and whole words.
  • Pick a text box, choose your handwriting, and type. Choose pen color, thickness and neatness.
  • With mail merge, every envelope comes out a little different, the way real handwriting does.
  • Save up to five handwritings, print and cursive, even your real signature.
  • It all runs on your computer. No AI service or internet is needed for handwriting.

Questions

Do I need Microsoft Publisher installed?

No. PageStudio is its own program and opens .pub files without Publisher. Very old files from Publisher 2001 and earlier aren't supported.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once in the Microsoft Store and keep it, with a 15-day free trial first.

Does it need the internet?

No. Designing, printing, handwriting and background removal all work offline. Only the optional Canva and Claude features, and pictures from web addresses, go online.

Do the AI features cost extra?

They're optional. The Claude assistant uses your own Claude desktop app or Anthropic API key, and Canva uses your own Canva account, each billed by that company if they charge. Handwriting doesn't use any AI service.

Does it work on a Mac or Chromebook?

PageStudio runs on Windows PCs. The free phone viewers let Android phones and iPhones view your publications.

Are my files private?

Yes. Your files and handwriting stay on your computer. PageStudio doesn't collect or send them anywhere. See the privacy policy.
